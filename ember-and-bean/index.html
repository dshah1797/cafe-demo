Our Story

A room built for lingering

Ember & Bean began with a simple idea: the best café is the one you do not want to leave.

We imagine a place where beans are chosen for flavour first, roasted in small batches and pulled with patience. Where bread is baked before the doors open, eggs come from farms we know by name, and fruit changes with the season.

It is also a place for people. Soft light, good chairs, a table for one book or ten friends, and a team who remembers how you take your coffee.

  • Considered coffeeTraceable beans, measured and brewed with care.
  • Honest ingredientsFresh, local where possible, never fussy.
  • Unhurried hospitalityNo rush, no timers. Just a good table.
